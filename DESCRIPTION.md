# Five Second Overlay

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

**A quiet mark on your mana bar for the five-second rule.** After a mana spell lands, a faint highlight covers the bar and shrinks away over five seconds, right to left. When it is gone, mana is regenerating again.

It starts when the spell finishes, or when a channel begins. Starting a cast is not enough, and interrupting the cast does not leave the highlight up. Wand shots do not count.

The highlight prefers an EllesmereUI resource bar, then an Ellesmere unit frame, then the normal player mana bar. Color defaults to white. Opacity and inset are in the settings. The minimap button is off until you turn it on.

`/fso` opens settings. So does the addon compartment.
